The RBI AppSec Compliance Reporting Gap

RBI regulated entities need more than periodic scan reports. They need continuous evidence that applications, APIs, software components, third-party dependencies, and remediation workflows are being governed and verified.

The problem is that AppSec evidence is scattered across scanners, DevOps tools, spreadsheets, service desks, VAPT reports, cloud platforms, and audit folders. OpsMx turns that fragmented evidence into one regulator-ready reporting workflow.
