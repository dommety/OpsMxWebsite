The CRA Compliance Reporting Gap

The EU Cyber Resilience Act changes product security from a point-in-time certification exercise into an ongoing lifecycle obligation. Organizations need evidence for secure development, vulnerability handling, software updates, SBOM transparency, and post-market risk management.

The challenge is that CRA evidence is scattered across code repositories, scanners, SBOM tools, vulnerability databases, service desks, AI workflows, and release systems. OpsMx turns that fragmented evidence into one continuous CRA compliance reporting workflow.

"CRA compliance is not just producing an SBOM. It is proving that product security risks are discovered, prioritized, remediated, verified, and reported throughout the software lifecycle."
